This part teaches how Strata decides what a surface looks like under light, from a lamp's numbers to the pixel. It starts with real units and the material model, then builds the reflection of light and measures it with the debug views. The turning point is the lights: once points, spots and area lights stand, the pages add shadows. The last pages light a whole scene with sky, atmosphere and fog, and end with clear coat and glass. At the end you can compute a lamp's light, shade a pixel by hand and tune a shadow.
