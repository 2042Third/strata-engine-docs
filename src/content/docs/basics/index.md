This part gives you what the rest of the book assumes: how a computer runs a program, and how you write, debug and build one. It starts with the processor, tools, programming, debugging and git. Numbers and data structures follow, then the C++ that Strata uses, the build, bytes and data formats. The mathematics runs from vectors and matrices to cameras, rotations, change over time, color, signals, chance and the sphere, and threads close the part. At its end you can read Strata's C++ and compute by hand what later parts need.
