This part teaches how Strata turns the light of a frame into the picture a player sees. The pages start with why a picture needs passes, then cover exposure, pre-exposure, the tone curve, grading, the encoding and lens effects. The turning point is anti-aliasing, where they pass from one frame to the history of many. Motion blur and depth of field then work on that result, and the last pages cover quality choices and a tutorial. At the end you can find the pass behind a fault, compute what a stage does to a value and add an effect.
