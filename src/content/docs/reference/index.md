This part holds the facts of Strata in tables, for a reader who needs an exact name, number or limit. It starts with the script interface, the commands, the components and the settings: the names you type or set. Pages on single subsystems follow, from the scene to worlds, then the editor, its automation and the engine's core. The last pages give the build, the measured costs, a cheat sheet, the notation, the glossary and two indexes. At the end you can find the command, property, setting, limit or budget that a task needs.
