This book teaches two things with one engine, Strata. It shows you how to make a game with Strata's editor, from an
empty project to a game you can give to other people. It also shows you how an engine like Strata works inside, so that
you can read its code and change it safely.

You do not need to know anything to start. Every page says which pages to read first, and those pages are in this book.

## Choose your track

A track is an order of reading: one for game makers, one for readers who start from zero, and one for engine builders.
[How to read this book](start/how-to-read.md) describes the three tracks and lets you choose one. When you follow a
track, the "previous" and "next" links at the bottom of every page follow it.

## What a page looks like

Every page starts with a strip that says what you will be able to do after it and which pages to read first. The strip
also says what kind of page it is and which engine version it was checked against. Every page ends with a summary, questions with hidden
answers, exercises and the files in the engine where the subject lives.

These pages show each kind:

- [The tools: the terminal, files, a code editor and git](basics/tools.md), a primer: a lesson from zero, with a Test
  out box for readers who know it already.
- [Installing Strata and the C++ build tools](start/install.md), a how-to: steps to a goal.
- [Time: delta time, the fixed step and time scale](core/time.md), a concept: a problem and how engines solve it.
- [Drawing between fixed steps](world/interpolation.md), internals: how Strata's code does it, quoted from the engine.
- [Your first script](make/first-script.md), a tutorial: a small game, step by step.
- [Glossary](reference/glossary.md), a reference: every term of the book with its definition.
