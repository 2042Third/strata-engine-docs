# Notes: `make/review`

## Pages each exercise needs

1. Basic: `make/transforms-units`, `make/scenes-entities`, `make/prefabs` (child position under a turned and scaled parent, overrides, apply).
2. Basic: `make/lighting`, `make/look` (lux and stops, manual exposure, ACES against Filmic, auto exposure limit, the camera's order).
3. Medium: `make/script-loop`, `make/physics`, `make/timers-events` (accumulator steps, force in a fixed step, time scale).
4. Medium: `make/physics`, `make/sound` (kinematic body, impact volume with its clamp, early request, inverse gain).
5. Medium: `make/hud`, `make/project-coin-run` (canvas scale and match, bar length, fill amount, time scale 0).
6. Medium: `make/timers-events`, `make/project-tetris`, `make/automated-playtests` (`KeyRepeat`, level from lines, steps against frames, exit code).
7. Medium: `make/commands`, `make/with-an-agent`, `make/project-files` (undo steps, atomic batch, forced scene change).
8. Hard: `make/gameplay-math`, `make/characters-cameras`, `make/project-platformer` (walk direction, yaw, shortest turn, smoothing weight, drawn position, camera arm, coyote time).
9. Hard: `make/performance`, `make/look`, `make/quality` (frame time, what bounds a frame, cap, cost of effects, draw calls).
10. Hard: `make/export`, `make/levels-saves`, `make/project-platformer`, `make/project-coin-run` (pruned export, asset field, Always Include, log path, saves, `ParseWhole`).

## Not covered

No exercise draws on `make/editor-tour`, `make/assets`, `make/materials`, `make/first-script`, `make/scripting-scene`, `make/input`, `make/script-fields`, `make/patterns`, `make/recipes`, `make/script-builds`, `make/animation`, `make/menus`, `make/lamps`, `make/reflections`, `make/gi`, `make/large-worlds`, `make/samples` or `make/designing-a-game`; their questions stay on their own pages.
