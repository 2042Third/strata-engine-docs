# Notes: indirect/ao

## New terms

- obscurance: the older darkening of a point that grows with the nearness of blocking surfaces and ignores angles, against visibility, which counts the cosine-weighted free share of the hemisphere. Home: indirect/ao. Aliases: none. (Used in plain prose, not bold; ask only if the clerk wants it as a term.)
