## Pages each exercise needs

1. Basic: `reference/build`, `reference/editor` (ctest label filter, `--no-gpu`, `--quit-after-commands`, exit codes 1 and 3, labels `fuzz` and `perf`).
2. Basic: `reference/assets`, `reference/worlds-runtime` (budget shares, headless pools, `--asset-budget-mb`, upload step and per-frame limits, exit code 3).
3. Basic: `reference/physics`, `reference/components` (capsule scale rules, mass minimum, rigid body with controller, impulse unit).
4. Medium: `reference/automation`, `reference/editor`, `reference/commands` (idle timeout of `mcp`, `--idle-timeout`, client limit, sign-in rules, error codes, `asset.delete` and `force`).
5. Medium: `reference/settings`, `reference/physics`, `reference/scene` (`Scene.Timing`, step cap at a project rate, `ContactHertz` ceiling, `project.setSettings`).
6. Medium: `reference/physics`, `reference/worlds-runtime` (automatic room and `reservedMB`, world extents, `c_MaxBodyCoordinate`, waiting counters).
7. Medium: `reference/performance`, `reference/build` (perf label, canary metric, budget rule, ratchet, provisional metrics).
8. Hard: `reference/renderer`, `reference/settings`, `reference/worlds-runtime`, `reference/editor` (quality rows by stage and memory, players' rows, `--quality`, Custom range of `ContactShadows`).
9. Hard: `reference/ui`, `reference/performance` (layout cache of four, `FullRebuilds`, atlas limit, `Perf.GameUI` metrics).
