## What was checked

- Homes (step 1): all 321 items of `where/render-scene.tsv` (feature 100, concept 22, code 32, figure 31, rule 24, hands-on
  18, term 86, open 8). 268 are homed by `draw`, 53 elsewhere (`gpu` 21, `light` 12, `animation` 8, `make-b2` 4,
  `basics-b` 3, `basics-a` 1, `make-a` 1, `make-b1` 1), 2 are `nowhere`. I read every page section of `draw.md` in full
  (Covers, Leaves out, Items, Assumes), the Covers, Leaves out and Items of `light/sun-shadows-1`, the Covers and Leaves
  out of `light/local-shadows` and `make/performance`, and every home row of other units that names a `render-scene` item.
  One home is misplaced (finding 9). The other 52 homes outside `draw` are where the one-home table of `book.md` puts the
  subject. Rule D was applied to the `make` pages and `draw/entities-to-draws`: no duplicate angle found.
- Thin homes (step 2): every concept (22), rule (24), hands-on (18) and figure (31), and the long feature rows of 1.1, 1.4,
  1.6, 1.7, 1.8 and 1.10, against the home row and the page's Covers. Findings 1, 2, 6 and 11. The rest are taken whole.
- Nowhere (step 3): `render-scene:706` and `render-scene:718` are surveyor's notes; the book needs neither. The ranges
  that `render-scene:706` lists as unread hide the gap of finding 1, which `render-direct` also left unread.
- Missing (step 4): a script (`docs-work/B/critic-render-scene/sweep.py`) listed 880 names at the pin: types, public
  functions and constants of `RenderScene.h`, `RenderSceneGPU.h`, `SpatialIndex.h`, `AssetResolveCache.h`,
  `SceneDrawKey.h`, `GPUSceneData.h`, `LODSelection.h`, `GeometryPool.h`, `SceneVertexLayouts.h`, `SceneRenderData.h`; every
  `SceneRenderer::` function of `SceneRendererExtraction.cpp` and `SceneRendererGPUDriven.cpp`; the functions and
  defines of the area's 16 shader files; the fields of `SceneRendererSpecification`. It grepped each in all inventories
  and unit files. 601 had no inventory hit. I read the code behind the ones that are behaviour rather than accessors,
  layout constants or names only a generated table carries (`SceneRendererSpecification` fields such as
  `MaxDrawsPerIndirectCall`, the stats). Also swept by hand: the registered shader entries (`Strata/CMakeLists.txt:80-201`),
  the `renderStats` keys (they match the inventory), the quality rows, the GPU and CPU profiler stages (covered by
  `render-core`), the perf budgets of `StrataTests/Perf/Budgets.json` that concern the area, and the test suites of 14 test
  files. Findings 3, 4, 7 and 10 come from these sweeps; finding 1 adds `GatherShadowDraws` and the GPU shadow culling.
- Order: the Assumes of every `draw` page against what its Covers uses (finding 5).
