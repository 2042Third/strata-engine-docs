## What was checked

- Homes (step 1): all 398 items of where/gamedev.tsv, each against the page's title, kind and tracks in pages.tsv. Only one home is misplaced (finding 8). Rule D splits are sound: code 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 30 and hands-on E1 to E9 on track E pages; their maker sides are on make pages.
- Thin homes (step 2): every concept (25), code excerpt (30), figure (53), rule (32), hands-on (25) and open item (7). Also every feature row of sections 1.1 to 1.20, including the long entries of 1.3, 1.8, 1.13, 1.14, 1.16, 1.18 and 1.20. I compared each with its home's takes and its other rows, and followed split parts to their other pages: the chips of 1.3, the debug views, G7's lantern, G4's material half, G12's sound budget, concept 19's voice budget (`make/recipes`), concept 20's navigation (`make/menus`) and the Preferences. Findings 1 and 9 are what no page takes.
- Nowhere (step 3): both items (`gamedev:691` and `:708`) are notes about the survey, so the book needs neither.
- Sweeps at the pin (step 4), each made with a script in my scratch directory, then judged by reading the code:
  - the headings of the ten Manual files against the inventories;
  - the 16 options of the editor and the 12 of the runtime (all listed);
  - the menu items of EditorLayer.cpp and EditorActions.cpp, and the Content Browser's menus;
  - the 8 settings sections;
  - the 337 function names of the SDK headers (127 appear in no inventory; all belong to families the inventories list, apart from the map switch of finding 4);
  - the properties of the 42 components of ComponentSchema.json against the make pages (which gave finding 3);
  - the 43 script files of the samples against the plan;
  - the Pitfalls table of the make-a-game skill and the scripting skill's Rules and building blocks;
  - PackageReadme's headings;
  - the editor's shortcuts.
  The command sweep's pattern caught 109 of the 154 registrations; the 17 names it found in no inventory are names only, which `reference/commands` carries.
- Other checks:
  - A script listed every gamedev item that a page uses before its home page. Every hit is a tutorial's first step, a link, or covered by an Assumes row, apart from finding 7.
  - A script paired each term with same-named terms of other inventories, and with the glossary seed (finding 11).
- Stale items (step 5): I spot-checked the panel set (13), the asset types (13), the runtime's exit codes, the template's values, the one-shot wait (0.25 s), the save-name rules, the gamepads (4), the manifest version (5) and the Tetris line counts. All agree with the pin apart from finding 6.
