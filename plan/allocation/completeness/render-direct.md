## What was checked

- Homes (step 1): all 374 items of `where/render-direct.tsv` (370 home, 4 nowhere), judged against the pages' titles, kinds and tracks in `pages.tsv` and the Covers of all 18 `light` pages in allocation/light.md, which I read whole. Items passed elsewhere were judged by their take cells: `image/exposure`, `image/pre-exposure`, `image/why-post`, `indirect/ibl`, `indirect/probes`, `draw/mirrors-camera-relative`, `gpu/frame-tour`, `gpu/coordinates`, `gpu/readback-timing`, `basics/color`, `basics/math-probability`, `make/lighting`, `make/lamps`, `make/reflections`, `engineering/look-dev`, `engineering/test-kinds` and `reference/renderer`. Those are the only misplacements I found (findings 5, 15 and 18). I also used rule D against other inventories' items on the same subjects: light records (render-scene:49 against render-direct:80, no duplicate), sky presets and Extract Sun (gamedev, render-indirect and assets items, how-to against internals), the derivative rule (finding 4) and the glossary (finding 21).
- Thin homes (step 2): I compared every feature (115), concept (23), rule (30), hands-on (22), figure (50) and open (9) item with its home row and the home page's Covers. For code (37) and terms (88), I checked only the placement. The inventory's long entries (35, 74, 113, 123, 151, 170, the 23 concepts, rules 16, 18 and 30) were read in full against their rows.
- Nowhere (step 3): 811, 820, 835 and 841. Each one is a surveyor's note or a repeat (841 repeats 627, and `new:12` takes its other half). The book needs none of them. I used 811's list of unread files to steer the sweep of step 4.
- What the inventory itself misses (step 4), with scripts in the scratch directory `critic-render-direct/`:
  - The public names of 11 headers of the area (RenderLights, ShadowCascades, ShadowAtlasAllocator, ShadowView, Atmosphere, SkyPresets, TextureTable, BlueNoise, Material, MaterialData, HDRSun), grepped against every inventory, items table and allocation file.
  - The fields of `SceneRendererStats` and `SceneRendererSpecification`. These tables are generated, so they gave no finding.
  - Every registered property of the five components and `MeshRenderer.CastShadows`, with their inactive reasons.
  - The commands `lighting.*`, `material.*` and the `debugView` values of `viewport.*`.
  - The function names of 23 shaders of the area.
  - The headings and bold bullets of the guide's sections of the area (3955-4010, 4506-4910, 5297-5390, 5754-5830, 6071-6135).
  - The quality rows, Tools, the area's test files, and the script ABI and SDK for light or material names.
  - Searches for absent features: cookies, IES, light channels, lightmaps, clouds, stars and moon, vertex colors, anisotropy.
  - The bodies of `EnsureShadowMap`, `EnsureLocalShadowAtlas`, the caster filters, `Material::FromJson`, the glTF light import, the forward shader's main and occlusion, the multiple-scattering shader, and the code that times the `GPU.Cull` stage.
- Stale items (step 5): 121, 204-205 with 658, and 77 are reported. I spot-checked these numbers at the pin and found them correct: the cluster grid and caps, the atmosphere table sizes and radii, the storage scale, the static asserts for 144, 64 and 96 bytes, `c_MaxShadowedLocalLights`, `c_LocalShadowFadeFrames`, `LocalShadowViewsPerFrame`, `SunTransmittance`, the sized-source formula, and the quality rows.
- I had no findings beyond the 22 in the table.
