## What was checked

- Homes (step 1): all 366 items of `prep/where/scene-ecs.tsv`. 360 have a home: 309 in `world` and 51 elsewhere (core 12,
  editor-ui 7, editor 6, make 9, worlds 4, assets 3, basics 5, physics 3, gpu 2). Six are `nowhere`. I judged every
  home against the page's title and kind in `pages.tsv`, and read the Covers of every `world` page and of
  `gpu/debug-drawing`, `physics/collision-layers`, `worlds/game-identity`, `core/time`, `make/project-files` and
  `make/physics`. The only real misplacement is the split destruction (finding 3). The other homes are where a reader
  would look. The how-to and internals pairs (prefabs, interpolation, timing, collision layers, sub-scenes) take
  different angles, as rule D allows. `scene-ecs:215-216` and `692-693` sit on the same page as their twins in
  worlds-runtime (`worlds/game-identity`), so they need only the glossary merge that coverage.md names.
- Thin homes (step 2): every concept (25), figure (37), rule (31), hands-on (23) and code (35) item, and the long
  features 34, 62, 76, 84, 107, 157, 158, 161, 173, 174, 181, 194, 204, 212 and 218. I compared each inventory entry
  with its home row and the home page's Covers. Each code row's place was checked by `check_allocation.py code`, not by
  me. All 80 terms were checked by script against the seed and every other inventory's terms (findings 7 and 8).
- Nowhere (step 3): all six (742, 745, 753, 755, 758, 761) are notes of the surveyor or repeats; none is content. I
  took the list of files that 745 says the surveyor did not read as a guide for step 4.
- The inventory's gaps (step 4): a script listed the classes, functions and constants of the 40 headers of the area at
  the pin (`Scene` 27, `Reflection` 7, `Project` 4, `Core/SettingsRegistry.h`, `Core/ProjectSettings.h`): 911 names.
  361 of them appear in no file of the plan and 27 only in the units' files (scratch: `sweep.py`, `sweep-out.txt`). I
  judged the misses by reading the headers whole, and the code behind them where a header pointed to behaviour:
  `OnUpdateRuntime`, `DecomposeTransform`, `SetWorldTransform`, `GetAbsentSoftConstraints` and its log line, the cooked
  reader's mapping of properties, `EntitySink`'s parent links and `AmortizedCheck`. Most misses are accessors,
  private helpers or names that only the generated reference pages list. Also checked: the settings sections at the
  pin (8; the inventory's list matches), the 42 registered components (`ComponentSchema.json`), the built-in update
  order, the 15 service interfaces with their registrations, the Scene commands, EnTT 3.16 and its page sizes.
- Stale items (step 5): the decomposition claim (finding 1) and the missing activity version (finding 5). I confirmed
  three matters that coverage.md already lists and add only evidence. The comment of `SceneServices.h` names six of
  PhysicsSystem's services, but `Physics/PhysicsSystem.cpp:61-67` registers seven (capstone's question). Strata's UUID
  is 64-bit. Units assume signals from `world/ecs`; `editor-ui/lists` (editor-ui.md:572) is a third such row, beside
  the two the world unit names. The inventory's "23 sources" is 24 at the pin, and its "213 test cases" counts 245
  `TEST_CASE`s now; neither reaches a page.
- Totals: 20 findings: 1 must, 7 should, 12 could. By kind: 2 stale-item, 5 thin-home, 4 missing-item, 3 order,
  2 duplicate, 4 other. I left out smaller matters that I judged to be no finding: FloatToJson writes non-finite values
  as 0, the unused `NoCopy` flag, and that removing a required component is allowed (the editor-ui inventory covers
  that).
