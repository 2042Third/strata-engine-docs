## What was checked

- Step 1, homes. I checked all 344 items of `where/foundation.tsv` (337 `home`, 7 `nowhere`) against their home page's row in `pages.tsv` and in the outline. I read all sixteen `core` sections of `allocation/core.md` whole (Covers, Leaves out, Fit, Items, Assumes), with its Passed on and Questions, and `reference/core` in `allocation/reference.md`. For homes outside `core` I read the take cells, and also the Covers of `automation/sessions` and the rows of `editor.md:880`, `draw.md:374`, `make-a.md:1522` and `make-b1.md:746`. One real misplacement remains (finding 4). The homes that rule D allows (asserts on `core/conventions` and `core/debugging`, the log on `core/conventions` and `core/debugging`, the fixed step on `core/time` and `world/systems`) are different angles and are not findings.
- Step 2, thin homes. I compared the inventory's text with the home rows and the Covers for every item of these types: all 26 concepts, 31 code items, 26 figures (diagrams and screenshots), 26 rules and 24 hands-on items. I did the same for every feature of sections 1.1 to 1.8 and 1.11 (the long entries: 33, 43, 45, 46, 52, 65, 67, 68, 71, 80, 81, 96, 103-106, 120-122, 184, 187). For each term I checked only that it has a home and that multi-term entries name every term. Two thin homes came out of this (findings 2 and 8). Finding 10 is a smaller one.
- Step 3, nowhere. I read all 7 `nowhere` items. `foundation:369` is held by `scene-ecs:388` on `world/systems` (`Scene.cpp:2199-2335`, with time dilation and the cap). `foundation:580` is held by `render-core:692` on `gpu/how-a-gpu-frame-works`. `foundation:708`'s two places exist: `editor-ui:116-119` on `editor-ui/status` and `worlds-runtime:129` on `worlds/runtime`. The four notes of the survey (688, 695, 696, 707) are not content. None is a nowhere-wrong.
- Step 4, sweeps at the pin (822a7a46). `critic-foundation/sweep.py` listed the classes, functions, macros and `c_` constants of every header in `Core`, `Events`, `Math`, `Engine`, `Platform/GLFW`, `Platform/Windows` and `Platform/Vulkan`. 492 of those names appear in no inventory and no item table. I read those headers to judge them: almost all are getters or internals, and the Vulkan bindless and debug-message helpers belong to `render-core`. I also checked:
  - the 13 `STRATA_*` environment variables read by `Strata/src`, `StrataEditor`, `StrataRuntime` and `StrataCLI`: all are in inventories;
  - every `ST_PROFILE_STAGE` name and every scene-system `ProfileStage`: 17 CPU stages (finding 3);
  - every exit code the programs set (findings 7 and 11);
  - every registry that `RegisterBuiltinModules` opens, and which of them freeze (finding 1);
  - the 36 static functions of `Platform.h` against the groups of `foundation:120`: all are covered;
  - the 15 event types and where each is used: file drop is `editor-ui:121`, typed keys are ImGui's;
  - the test suites of `StrataTests/src/Core`, `Engine`, `Math` and `Platform`, by case name;
  - the "Frame profiler", "Composition root and registries" and "Threading model" sections of `Docs/Architecture.md`;
  - `Window.h` and `GLFWWindow.cpp` for the window modes, `Log.cpp` and `BackgroundLogSink.h` for the log, `Application.cpp` for `Run`, `RunFrame`, the constructor and `ReportFatalError`, `EntryPoint.h`, and the crash guard's scope.
- Step 5, stale items. I checked these numbers at the pin, and all are as the inventory says: 19 layers (the erratum `new:8` exists), 12 built-in stages, 15 event types, 9 module rows, the pool sizes (I/O 2, `c_FrameHardwareThreads` 4), 64 stages, a ring of 600, a queue of 65,536, the 0.25 s clamp, the 16 ms skip, exit codes 1, 3 and 130, and the profiler's cost of about 0.6 µs (`StrataTests/Perf/Budgets.json:48`).
