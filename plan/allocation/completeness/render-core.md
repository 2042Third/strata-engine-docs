## What was checked

- Homes (step 1): all 376 items of `prep/where/render-core.tsv` (131 features, 32 concepts, 33 code, 26 figures, 37 rules,
  19 hands-on, 89 terms, 9 open). I judged each against its page's outline row in `prep/pages.tsv`, and against the Covers
  and Leaves out of all 15 `gpu` pages in `allocation/gpu.md`. For the 26 items homed outside `gpu`, I used their take
  cells, plus the Covers of `assets/models-gltf` and `core/profiling` where needed. I applied rule D. Only `render-core:214`
  and `render-core:708` (findings 9 and 16) are misplaced. I also noted one duplicate angle (finding 15).
- Thin homes (step 2): every concept (32), code item (33, range against inventory), figure (26), rule (37) and hands-on
  (19). Also the long features: 28-46, 52-54, 61-67, 73-78, 84-88, 94-102, 110-113, 119-131, 137-144, 180, 201-204,
  210-214, 220-226, 232-239, 245-247. Findings 2, 6, 10 and 14 came from this step.
- Nowhere (step 3): all 7 (`465`, `532`, `825`, `833`, `835`, `843`, `847`). Each repeats a homed item or is a
  surveyor's note: `465` repeats `foundation:370` on `core/math`, `532` repeats `render-scene:450`, `835` is
  `editor-ui:40` on `editor-ui/imgui-backends`. The book needs none of them.
- Sweeps at the pin (step 4):
  - every file of `Strata/src/Strata/Renderer` (133) and `Strata/src/Platform/Vulkan` (8) against the inventory's names
    (all accounted for);
  - the classes, functions and `c_` constants of the 40 headers this area owns, grepped in `inventory/*.md` and
    `allocation/items/*.tsv`, then read where a name pointed to a behaviour;
  - all 130 shader files (89 entries + 25 variants confirmed);
  - the editor's and runtime's `c_Arguments`;
  - the editor commands that touch rendering (all owned by the editor and automation areas);
  - environment reads in the renderer (none);
  - `GraphicsModuleSpecification`, `RendererSpecification`, `SceneRendererSpecification` and `SceneRenderOptions` (field
    names are generated; their behaviours were grepped in the plan);
  - the guide's sections "macOS and MoltenVK", "GPU tests", "Streaming and uploads", "Overlays", "Text and fonts",
    "Readback", "GPU timing";
  - NVRHI's Vulkan pipeline cache.
- Stale items (step 5), spot checks against the pin: struct sizes, stage names, the profiler ring, font, atlas, staging,
  bindless and pool constants, the deferred-release budget, the swap chain's formats and modes, the test names the
  hands-on cite, and the Roadmap's single RenderDoc line. All matched except the two counts in finding 17.
- More findings than the 17 above: none of weight. I also checked and dropped, because a page or another inventory
