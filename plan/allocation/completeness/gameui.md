## What was checked

- Homes (step 1): every one of the 348 items of `where/gameui.tsv`, against the outline rows in `pages.tsv` and the Covers paragraphs of the six `ui` pages, `make/hud`, `make/menus`, `gpu/text`, `gpu/debug-drawing`, `editor-ui/scene-view` and `reference/ui`. All 40 items homed outside part `ui` were read with their takes. One real misplacement (finding 8) and one duplicate (finding 18). Rule D pairs (`make/hud` and `ui/layout` on anchors; `make/menus` and `ui/input` on widgets; `reference/script-api` and `ui/input` on the SDK) take different angles and are not findings.
- Thin homes (step 2): every item of type concept (23), code (31), figure (38), rule (24), hands-on (15) and open (9), each compared with its inventory text, as were the long feature rows: 81, 142-158, 166-170, 176-195, 201-206 and 214-220. The evidence of the unit's new items `new:5`, `new:6` (code), `new:12` (erratum) and `new:13` (rule) was read again at the pin and holds. Findings 1, 4, 10, 14, 16 and 17 come from this step.
- Nowhere (step 3): all 19. The 17 terms each repeat a term homed elsewhere; I looked up all 17 twins in `where/*.tsv` and each has a home with the same meaning. The 2 opens are notes about the survey. None is wrong.
- Missing (step 4): a script (`critic-gameui/sweep_names.py`) listed the classes, structs, enums, functions and constants of the 8 GameUI headers and grepped each in `inventory/*.md` and `items/*.tsv`. Of the 105 names it missed, most are internal or come only from generated lists. I judged the rest by reading the code: `GameUISystem.cpp` (update, pointer, navigation, editing, `IUIControl`), `GameUIScene.cpp` (changes, parts, input screen, canvas matrix), `GameUIRenderer.{h,cpp}` (Prepare, stats, failures), `GameUIQuads.cpp` indices, `GameUIRenderData.h`, `GameUIComponents.{h,cpp}` (every registration and inactive reason), `SceneOverlay.h`, `SceneServices.h` (UI part), `UIActions.{h,cpp}`, `InputActionSet.cpp` defaults, `Input.h` capture, the SDK's `UI.h`, `ScriptSystem::OnUIEvent`, `EditorUICommands.cpp` descriptions, `CanvasEdit`, `GameUIPresets.cpp` (the input field), the Manual's `GameUI.md` (whole), the scripting skill's Game UI section, the Roadmap and the 0.4.0 to 0.7.0 notes on game UI, the test case names of every GameUI suite, and `Budgets.json`. The module has no settings section, debug view or command-line option. Findings 3, 5, 6, 9, 12 and 13 come from this step.
- Stale (step 5), as met: the eleven components (`new:2` holds), 17 files and 6,668 lines, 15 UI host functions, the shader entries at `CMakeLists.txt:171-172`, the repeat constants, the navigation weight, the default UI bindings, the Third-Person menu's components (ten of eleven) and camera, and no input field in any sample. Findings 2, 11, 15 and 16 are the claims the pin contradicts.
- Order: the ui pages' Assumes rows outside their closure (`assumes.tsv`) are all `helps`, and the two order questions are already in coverage.md; no new order finding.
