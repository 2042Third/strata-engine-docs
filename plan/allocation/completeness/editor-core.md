## What was checked

- What I read: the contract and the inventory in full (808 lines). Also `where/editor-core.tsv` (391 items: 387 with a home, 4 nowhere) and the 19 page sections of `allocation/editor.md` (Covers, Leaves out, Items, Passed on, Questions). Also the outline rows `outline-4.md:75-93`, the order of track E in `tracks.md`, sections 1-5 and 8 of `coverage.md` (editor, editor-ui) and `from-workflow-a.md` "final.forPlanner".
- Homes (step 1): I checked all 391 homes against the outline rows and the Covers. That includes the 36 homes outside the unit: 12 on `editor-ui`, 9 on `world`, 5 on `make-a`, 3 on `core`, 3 on `gpu`, and one each on `assets`, `audio-input`, `draw` and `light`. I also checked the game makers' hands-on 604, 606, 609, 611, 613, 614, 615 and 618 against `make-a.md` and `make-b2.md`. Their track G angle is covered (rule D).
- Thin homes (step 2): I compared every concept (22), code excerpt (35, all homed, the ten must-haves among them), figure (35), rule (26) and hands-on (17) with its home row and Covers. I did the same for the long feature rows of 1.1-1.12. I compared all 93 terms by script with the terms of the other inventories (`terms.py` in my scratch directory).
- Nowhere (step 3): I read the four nowhere items (789, 797, 807, 808). All four are notes about the survey. For 808, the statement that a command a game relies on gets a step in the feature test holds at the pin (`Docs/DeveloperGuide.md:850`). The capture logic that 789 left unread is finding 1.
- Sweeps (step 4), by scripts in my scratch directory:
  - All 153 commands, from the kind lists of `CommandJournalTests.cpp` plus the two of the UI (`cmds.py`). The 19 commands that no inventory names in full appear in the group map in short form; I read the copy, paste and duplicate commands (finding 5).
  - All 62 headers of `StrataEditor/src/Editor` (`headers.py`). Eleven appear in no allocation file. They belong to other areas, except `EditorPreferences` (finding 7).
  - The public functions of 17 core headers that no plan file names (`methods.py`). I read the ones that look like behaviour (`StopOnScriptFault`, `NotifyImportFailures`, `IsAnnouncedLogEntry`).
  - The 16 command-line options of `c_Arguments`. The reference generates their table, so the inventory's list of 10 is no finding, except the headless definition (finding 3).
  - The headings of the developer guide's Editor section (2730-3852). All appear in the inventory's list. I read lines 3091-3135, 3176-3260 and 3610-3692 in full against the plan. I also checked the fields of `EditorContextSpecification`, `SceneRecovery`'s comments and limits, and `EditorLayer::OnUpdate`.
- Stale claims (step 5): these values match the pin:
  - 151 commands (63 Edit, 46 Query, 42 Session), and the lists of 20, 44 and 10.
  - Undo capacity 512; `c_Version` 11; 12 recent projects; usage exit code 3.
  - Recovery timing and limits: 2 s, 10 s, 256, 20 and 8.
  - 4 active toasts; the journal's 2,000 entries, 8 MB and 256 KiB; batches of 1,000 steps; `c_SettleFrames` 12.
  - The command group map, command by command.
- Stale claims I found: `editor.perf` (finding 2) and the headless definition (finding 3). Also, the inventory's "How completeness was checked" counts 70 headers, while the pin has 62 headers among 142 files. That text is not an item.
- Not listed because they weigh less:
  - Headless editors run at most 60 frames per second.
  - A paused game stays paused through a scene switch, with its pending steps.
  - `EditorCommandRunner::CancelAll` runs only at shutdown, and the page says only "cancels what is pending".
  - `unrepeatableEdits` in the replay export.
