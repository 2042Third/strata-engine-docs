## What was checked

- Homes (step 1): all 339 items of `prep/where/automation.tsv` (312 home, 27 nowhere), each against its page's row in
  `pages.tsv`. I read the Covers of all six `automation/*` pages and of `editor/commands`, `editor/deferred-commands`,
  `editor/journal`, `make/commands` and `make/with-an-agent`. I grepped the rows that name automation items in
  `editor-ui.md`, `reference.md`, `core.md`, `basics-a.md`, `appendix.md`, `capstone.md` and `make-c.md`. The passed-on
  homes (`editor/journal`, `editor/commands`, `editor/command-scripts`, `editor/deferred-commands`, the `editor-ui` pages
  and the `make` pages) are where a reader looks. Rule D holds for the pairs of a `make` page and an E page. The one
  wrong home I found is finding 3.
- Thin homes (step 2): every item of the teaching types: 14 concepts, 32 code items (content only; the places were
  checked by the code check), 24 figures, 28 rules and 17 hands-on. I also checked the long feature entries of 1.3, 1.6,
  1.7, 1.9, 1.11 and 1.12 against the home rows and the Covers. The `MayDefer` half of `automation:685` is taken by
  `editor/deferred-commands`' exercise, and `TransferResponses` (concept 2.7) by the walk of `RunIteration` and the
  backpressure figure.
- Nowhere (step 3): all 27 rows. For each repeat I opened the item it names (`editor-core:355`, `:389`, `:463`, `:558`,
  `:575`, `:651-670`, `:688`, `:691`; `foundation:572`, `:588`, `:626`, `:629`, `:630`): all are sound. `automation:829`,
  `:836` and `:850` are notes; the screenshot question is already in coverage.md §8.
- Sweeps at the pin (step 4):
  - `critic-automation/sweep_names.py` read 14 headers (six of `Network`, six of `StrataCLI/src/CLI`, `EditorAutomation.h`
    and `CommandJournal.h`) and grepped every class, function and constant in all inventories and item tables. 236 names
    are in no inventory, nearly all accessors. I read the rest in the code, which led to findings 2, 6 and 7.
  - The CLI's 19 options (`c_Options`) and its usage text, the editor's `c_Arguments`, and every `STRATA_*` variable in
    the source. Their names are carried by the generated program-options table and the hand-checked environment table.
  - The MCP methods the server handles, its generic tools and its toolset tables.
  - Every bullet of the guide's "Automation" section (`Docs/DeveloperGuide.md:6261-6409`).
  - SK-A sections 1, 2, 4 and 6 and "Repeat a session"; SK-G section 7.
  - The automation mentions of `Docs/Architecture.md`; the editor's idle throttling (taken by `editor-ui/status`); the
    interrupt handler (taken by `core/platform`).
- Findings by kind: wrong-home 1, thin-home 3, missing-item 4, stale-item 3, duplicate 3, nowhere-wrong 0, order 0.
- Dropped as already covered: the launch knobs `--wait-timeout`, `--stall-timeout` and `--no-wait-scan` (the generated
  options table plus the Covers of `automation/sessions`), and the OS sources of secure random bytes and process start
  times (`Platform::GenerateSecureRandom`, `GetProcessStartTime`; `core/platform` covers them by group).
- Stale items (step 5): I re-verified the constants, limits and names that the home rows quote (backpressure threshold,
  pre-auth limits, journal limits, chip and flash times, the 27 core commands, the protocol versions, the generic tools,
  the exercise names `GetGenericTools` and "Pipelined large responses"). They match the pin except findings 4, 13 and 14.
- `check_allocation.py check --unit automation` reports nothing, and `inbox automation` reports 0 items.
