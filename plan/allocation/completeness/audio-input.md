## What was checked

- Homes: all 347 items of `prep/where/audio-input.tsv` (345 homes, 2 nowhere). I compared each home with its page's outline row (outline-3.md:131-152, outline-4.md:87, :116, :119, :201) and with the Covers of all 13 pages of the unit. For passed items I read the Covers of `make/input`, `make/sound`, `make/menus`, `make/automated-playtests`, `editor/stepping`, `editor/play-mode`, `core/jobs` and `reference/audio-input`. I found no wrong home under rule D; the closest cases are findings 12, 13 and 17.
- Thin homes: every concept (23), code item (32), figure (36), rule (27), hands-on item (21) and open item (8), plus the long feature entries 79, 84, 123, 162, 165, 179 and 181, each compared with its home row and the page's Covers. Terms were checked only for twins across inventories (finding 18).
- Nowhere: both items. `audio-input:740` is a note on the survey. `audio-input:761` is a real cosmetic slip at `AudioSystem.cpp:867`. Both are rightly nowhere. Finding 4 comes from reading what item 740 says was skipped (`EditorInputCommands.cpp`).
- Sweep at the pin: a script (`docs-work/B/critic-audio-input/names.py`) listed the class, function and `c_` names of the 19 headers in `Strata/src/Strata/Audio` and `Strata/src/Strata/Input` and of the SDK's `Audio.h` and `Input.h`, then grepped each name in all inventories. The misses are getters, setters and constants that the generated reference carries. I read `AudioSystem.h`, `AudioEngine.h`, `Input.h` and `InputActions.h` whole, the GLFW feed, `InputActionSet.cpp` (schema, `FromJson`, name rules, lookups), the audio importer, `AudioClip.cpp`'s decoding, the frame's Input stage, and the editor's play start and stop. I also checked: commands (16, as the inventory says); host functions (40, as it says); registrations (two components, one settings section, one scene system, one loader); program options (no audio or input option beyond `--headless` and `--no-gpu`); test suites, including `Core.Application`, `Editor.Audio`, `Editor.Input`, `Editor.InputActions`, `Core.UIActions` and `Core.WindowBackend`, which the inventory does not name.
- Rules 492-494 against the vendored miniaudio 0.11.25: every voice and listener setter Strata calls writes atomically, except the listener's world up and `ma_sound_set_volume`, as the inventory says.
- Stale items: the inventory and the pin are the same commit (822a7a46). The numbers the pages state hold at the pin: the limits of `InputActionSet.h`, `c_MaxKeyCode` 512, the UI repeat timing, the perf budget (`Budgets.json:218`), and 929 mapping lines in GLFW's 1,001-line `mappings.h`. The file counts in the inventory's opening are wrong (the pin has 18 Audio files with 3,279 lines and 16 Input files with 3,951; the inventory says 12 with 2,371 and 17 with 3,859), but no page uses them. The wrong line numbers for `InputActions.h` are already among the unit's questions.
- By kind: 10 missing-item, 4 duplicate, 3 thin-home, 1 stale-item, 1 other. Nothing was cut to stay under 40.
