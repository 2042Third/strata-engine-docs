After joining what the three ways found twice, **39 merges**. None makes a page larger by more than about 150 words:
29 move term or rule items onto the page that already defines them, or trim a row's words, and change no words of
prose; 9 take an excerpt, a rule or part of a concept off a second page (each saves 100 to 300 words there; 6 of those
pages are `over` now); 1 adds about 30 words (the term "debug view" on `gpu/debugging`).

The method misses duplicates whose labels differ in words (way c compares labels only), names under four characters
(NDC was found by way a, not c), home rows that quote no place (way b reads places only), and pairs inside one
inventory (out of scope).
