A size or need question is marked "planner" where the unit asks for more than rule S or N gives. For those classes the
answers give only what goes beyond the bare size or Need. The full size and Need lists are in `sizes.md` and `needs.md`.

How the answers were checked. Every engine fact cited was read at the pin (`docs-pin`, 822a7a46). The Needs proposals
were run through `check_outline.py` on a scratch copy of the outline (`docs-work/B/questions-learner/mirror`), and all
passed. The closures come from a scratch script that copies coverage.md's rule (`closure.py`). The seed proposals were
run through `glossary/check_seed.py --seed` on scratch copies (`seed-new.json`, `seed-new2.json`, `seed-three.json`,
`seed-all.json`, `seed-proc.json`), and all passed. The term counts come from `count_terms.py` and the items tables.

What was not checked. Breakpoints on Linux and macOS were not run (basics-a-q5). The splits of rule S were not run
through the check as new pages. The units' word estimates were taken as written. Lines of the plan are cited as they
stood on 2026-11-10, while other agents were editing `site.md`, `process.md` and `pipelines.md`, so a line may have
moved. Each cited phrase can still be found with grep.
